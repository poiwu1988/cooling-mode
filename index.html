Cooling Mode Rules & Solar Correction Model

Operation ModeAmbient Weather Condition
MIXING COOLING Dry bulb < 32 °C, AND dew point ≤ 28 °C, AND RH > 80%
FREE COOLING Dry bulb < 32 °C, AND dew point ≤ 28 °C, AND RH ≤ 80%
TRIM COOLING 32 ≤ dry bulb < 45.5 °C, AND dew point ≤ 28 °C
RECIRCULATION Dry bulb ≥ 45.5 °C, OR dew point > 28 °C (typhoon not auto-detected)

Each time block uses the worst-case hourly mode (priority: Recirculation > Trim > Mixing > Free).

Solar correction (per CELL):
  Tadj = Tambient + OFFSET_MAX × max(0, cos(θsun − θfacing)) × DirectRadiation / 1000
where OFFSET_MAX = 3.0 °C and θfacing is the weather-station orientation (TPE03 all cells: NW = 315°; TPE05 CELL1/3: W = 270°; TPE05 CELL2/4: E = 90°). The correction only applies when the sun is above the horizon and on the facing side of the station.
